Equity Research & Valuation Samples

This repository contains original equity research and valuation work focused on the Electrical Equipment & Parts segment of the Industrials sector. The materials are intended to demonstrate fundamental analysis, financial modeling, and written investment research skills at a junior equity research / financial analyst level.

Repository Contents
1. nVent Electric (NVT) – Valuation Model

File: NVT.xlsx

An Excel-based valuation model analyzing nVent Electric using:

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) analysis

Comparable companies valuation

Historical financial statement analysis

Key operating assumptions, sensitivities, and valuation outputs

The model synthesizes company filings, peer benchmarks, and forward-looking assumptions to estimate intrinsic value and assess relative valuation within the peer group.

2. nVent Electric (NVT) – Equity Research Report

File: Equity Research Report - NVT.docx

A full-length equity research note presenting:

Company overview and business model

End-market exposure and competitive positioning

Financial performance and outlook

Valuation summary and risk–reward assessment

This report integrates the valuation work from the Excel model into a professional, sell-side–style research format.

3. Electrical Equipment & Parts Industry Outlook

File: Electrical Equipment and Parts Industry Report.docx

A sector-level research note covering:

Structural growth drivers, including data center investment, electrification, and utility infrastructure spending

Industry end-market dynamics and cyclicality

Comparative analysis of key publicly traded companies, including nVent (NVT), Vertiv (VRT), Hubbell (HUBB), and Acuity Brands (AYI)

Valuation dispersion and positioning across the peer group

The paper provides top-down context to support bottom-up equity analysis.

Purpose of This Repository

This repository was created to:

Demonstrate fundamental equity research skills

Showcase financial modeling and valuation proficiency

Illustrate the ability to translate analysis into clear, concise written investment research

Provide tangible work samples for equity research and financial analyst recruiting

All analysis is independent and for educational and demonstration purposes only.

Methodology & Sources

Company filings (10-Ks, 10-Qs, earnings releases)

Investor presentations and earnings call transcripts

Public market data (consensus estimates, trading multiples)

Industry research and public macro data

Assumptions and estimates are explicitly stated where applicable.
